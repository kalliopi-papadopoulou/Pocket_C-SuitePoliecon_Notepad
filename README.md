## Executive Overview

**`Pocket_C-SuitePoliecon_Notepad`** is an interactive quantitative calculating engine, policy simulation system, and visual Head-Up Display (HUD) architected for political-economic analysts, sovereign strategists, and C-suite decision-makers.

Built upon the **Modern Mainstream Economy (New Neoclassical Synthesis & New Keynesian Macroeconomics)**, the platform bridges short-run sticky-price demand management with long-run supply-side general equilibrium. It enables analysts to stress-test fiscal and monetary policy levers across **6 sovereign jurisdictions**, audit stakeholder and distributional impacts, and correlate domestic policy shifts directly with live global foreign exchange and sovereign bond market telemetry.

## Key Capabilities & System Modules

### 01 // Quantitative Metrics & Formula Solvers (10 Core Models)
Real-time algebraic solvers with interactive parameter telemetry inputs, equilibrium readouts, real-world policy scenarios, and participant justification matrices:
* **Price Elasticity of Demand (PED)** — Pigouvian behavioral taxation vs. Ramsey optimal revenue incidence.
* **GDP Identity (Expenditure Approach)** — Domestic absorption ($C + I + G$) vs. Net Export ($X - M$) twin-deficit auditing.
* **The Spending Multiplier ($k$)** — Open-economy fiscal traction adjusted for marginal tax clawbacks ($t$) and import leakage ($MPI$).
* **Quantity Theory of Money ($M \times V = P \times Y$)** — Broad money overhang vs. velocity shifts and real output capacity.
* **Money Multiplier ($mm$)** — Central bank Monetary Base ($MB$) transmission adjusted for excess reserve hoarding ($e$) and currency drain ($c$).
* **Taylor Rule Reaction Function** — Central bank nominal rate calibration enforcing the Taylor Principle ($\Delta i > \Delta \pi$).
* **Chain-Weighted Real GDP (Fisher Ideal Index)** — Geometric quantity chaining to eliminate relative-price substitution bias.
* **Social Cost of Carbon (SCC)** — 100-year discounted marginal climate damage valuation under Ramsey social discount rates.
* **Bank Leverage Ratio & Basel III Adequacy** — Non-risk-weighted Tier 1 equity backstops against systemic credit crunches.
* **Cobb-Douglas Production Function** — Supply-side potential output ($Y^*$), Marginal Product of Capital ($MPK$), and Total Factor Productivity ($A$).

### 02 // Interactive HUD Diagrams & Parametric Curves (31 Visual Models)
Dynamic vector (SVG) plots with live policy shock sliders and instant `.svg` vector / `.md` brief export:
* **Microeconomic & Welfare Foundations:** Production-Possibility Frontier (PPF), Marshallian Supply & Demand, Indifference Curves, Budget Constraint Line, Isocost & Isoquant Lines, Total/Average/Marginal Cost Curves, Perfect Competition, Monopoly Markup & Deadweight Loss, Kinked Demand Oligopoly, Edgeworth Box Contract Curve, Lorenz Curve (Gini), and Marginal Revenue Product (MRP).
* **Macroeconomic & Monetary Stabilization:** AS-AD (Demand-Pull vs. Cost-Push Stagflation), Keynesian Cross ($45^\circ$ Line), Consumption & Saving Functions, Investment Demand (MEI), Money Supply & Demand, Short-Run & Long-Run Phillips Curve (NAIRU), Laffer Curve, IS-LM Policy Mix, Solow Growth Model, Zero Lower Bound (ZLB) Liquidity Trap, and Taylor Rule Curve.
* **Quantitative Finance, Climate & Game Theory:** Sovereign Yield Curve (Term Structure), Quantitative Easing (QE) Central Bank Balance Sheet, Financial Accelerator / Credit Crunch Shifts, Marginal Abatement Cost (MAC) Curve, Climate Damage Function, Weitzman Cap-and-Trade vs. Carbon Tax, Repeated Prisoner’s Dilemma Game Tree, and New Trade Theory Returns-to-Scale Curve.

### 03 // Foundational Economic Laws (11) & Multi-Region Sovereign Indicators (17)
* **6 Sovereign Jurisdictions:** Instant calibration across **EU (ECB / Eurostat)**, **United States (Fed / BEA)**, **Switzerland (SNB / SECO)**, **United Kingdom (BoE / ONS)**, **Japan (BoJ / Cabinet Office)**, and **Singapore (MAS / MTI)**.
* **17 Editable Sovereign Indicators:** Nominal GDP, Real GDP Growth, CPI/HICP, PPI, GDP Deflator, Unemployment Rate, Labor Force Participation Rate (LFPR), Broad Money (M2/M4), Current Account Balance, Gini Coefficient, Green GDP (eaNDP), Social Cost of Carbon / ETS Price, Chained Real GDP, Bank CET1 Leverage, VIX/VSTOXX/JNIV Volatility, Human Development Index (HDI), and Total Factor Productivity (TFP).
* **11 Empirical Laws:** Downward-Sloping Demand, Diminishing Marginal Utility, Diminishing Returns, Law of Supply, Engel's Law, Okun's Law, Say's Law, Gresham's Law, Law of One Price (LOOP), Hotelling's Rule, and Goodhart's Law.

### 04 // Quantitative Finance & FX Telemetry Desk
* **Zero-Key Live ECB/Frankfurter Auto-Sync:** Automatically fetches daily interbank FX spot rates (`EUR/USD`, `USD/JPY`, `USD/SGD`, `USD/CHF`, `GBP/USD`) on application load and computes the live **ICE DXY Geometric Dollar Index** proxy.
* **Uncovered Interest Rate Parity (UIP) & Carry Trade Solver:** Correlates central bank policy rate differentials ($i_{\text{dom}} - i_{\text{for}}$) with 5Y Sovereign CDS risk premiums to project 12-month implied forward FX rates and fiscal dominance thresholds.

### 05 // Thematic Decision Matrix & Official Institutional Presets
* **Inline Taxonomy Extension Drawer:** Toggleable **New Neoclassical Synthesis & Mainstream Policy Taxonomy** embedded across all 69 instruments, detailing the *Economic Theme*, *Foundational Theory*, *Core Theoretical Mechanism*, and *Mainstream Policy Doctrine*.
* **Official 2026 & Archival Stress-Test Presets:** Pre-calibrated scenarios from the **IMF (WEO Oct 2026)**, **BIS (AER June 2026)**, **Federal Reserve (DFAST 2026)**, **NGFS Phase V / EU CBAM (2026)**, **BoJ GX / MAS S$NEER (2026)**, and archival **FRASER / ECB / SNB / BoE** historical shocks.

### 06 // C-Suite Advisory Dispatch & Google Calendar Integration
* Generates instant **Google Calendar deep-links** and downloadable **`.ics` calendar invitations** pre-populated with the analyst's active jurisdiction, formula output, diagram equilibrium, and live FX telemetry snapshot.

## Technology Stack & Deployment

* **Architecture:** Self-contained, single-file HTML5 / CSS3 / Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript (`index.html`) with zero external build dependencies or framework bloat.
* **Visual Engine:** Custom-engineered responsive CSS Grid/Flexbox obsidian-and-gold executive HUD paired with real-time parametric SVG vector plotting.
* **Data & Telemetry Layer:** Asynchronous REST `fetch()` integration with the open European Central Bank / Frankfurter FX API (`api.frankfurter.app`) and automatic `localStorage` offline caching.
* **Executive Export Suite:** Native client-side Blob serialization for one-click `.md` Executive Dossiers, `.csv` Sovereign Indicator tables, `.svg` vector diagrams, and `.ics` calendar dispatches.
* **Mobile-First Workflow:** Engineered and maintained via **Visual Studio Code for the Web (`vscode.dev` / `github.dev`)** and hosted on **GitHub Pages**.
